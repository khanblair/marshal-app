Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026. This applies to the Marshal desktop app and its background service ("Marshal"), and to this website.

The short version

  • Marshal runs on your computer. The Marshal project runs no server that your data passes through, and we do not receive your code, cards, chats, calendar, or email.
  • There is no Marshal account, and there is no analytics, advertising, or tracking code in the app or on this site.
  • Passwords, tokens, and API keys are kept in your operating system's keychain.
  • Google Calendar and Gmail are optional and read-only. Each asks for its own permission, and you can disconnect either at any time.

1. What stays on your computer

Your projects, cards, chat history, settings, and the working copies agents use are stored on your computer, in Marshal's own data folder. The tokens and keys you give Marshal are stored in your operating system's keychain. None of this is uploaded to the Marshal project.

You can delete cards and projects inside Marshal. To remove everything Marshal has stored, delete its data folder and remove its entries from your keychain.

2. Services you connect

Marshal talks directly from your computer to the services you choose. It does not pass your data through anyone else. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google Calendar and Gmail

These two connections are optional. Each is its own request for permission on Google's page, and each is read-only. The Marshal project never sees your Google data: it goes from Google to your computer, and stays there.

Where Google data can go from there

A card made from an email is an ordinary Marshal card, so what you do with a card applies to its text:

  • If you start the card, its text goes to the agent you chose and to that agent's model provider.
  • If the project is linked to a Trello board, or a pull request is opened for the card, the card's title or text can appear there.
  • If you turned on Telegram, Discord, or ntfy, the card's title can appear in a notice.

Marshal does these only because you set them up or started the card. Calendar events are not sent to any AI model or notice service. If a later version does anything else with Google data, this page will say so first.

You can also use your own Google Cloud client in Settings instead of Marshal's. The same limits above still apply to what Marshal does with the data.

4. Google API limited use

Marshal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, this means:

  • Marshal uses Google data only to provide the features described above, which you can see in the app.
  • Google data is not used for advertising, and is never sold.
  • Google data is not used to train AI models. Marshal does not train models.
  • No person at the Marshal project can read your Google data, because it never reaches us.

5. Disconnecting and deleting

  • In Marshal: Settings, Integrations, then Disconnect on a connection. This removes Marshal's stored tokens and settings for it from your computer. Cards already made from your email stay until you delete them.
  • At Google: When you disconnect the last Google connection in Marshal, Marshal asks Google to end the access. While Calendar or Gmail is still connected, the access stays so the other keeps working. If Google cannot be reached, or you want to check, open your Google account's third-party access page and remove Marshal there.
  • At GitHub: review or remove the App under authorized apps and installed apps.
  • Trello and the others: use that service's own settings to remove Marshal's access.

6. Security

Marshal's background service listens on your own computer only, not on the network. Tokens and keys are held in your operating system's keychain, not in plain files. Keep your computer's login secure, since Marshal's access to your accounts is as strong as your computer's.

Marshal is not meant for children.

7. This website

This site has no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits under its own privacy statement.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change to how Google data is used will be explained here before it takes effect.

Questions or requests: open an issue. Marshal is developed in the open at github.com/khanblair/marshal.
